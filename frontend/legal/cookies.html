Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies

Last Updated: 11 October 2026
📋 Summary

We use cookies for login sessions, analytics, and payment processing. You can disable them in your browser, but some features may not work.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

  • Session cookies — keep you logged in
  • CSRF tokens — prevent cross-site attacks
  • Language preference — remember your language choice

These cannot be disabled — the site won't work without them.

2.2 Analytics Cookies

  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage data (if enabled)
  • Page views, session duration — to improve UX

These can be disabled without affecting site functionality.

2.3 Payment Cookies

  • Razorpay cookies — for secure payment processing
  • Fraud detection — to prevent unauthorized transactions

These are required for payments to work.

2.4 Functional Cookies

  • User preferences — theme, layout choices
  • Form data — to remember your inputs

3. Third-Party Cookies

We use cookies from:

  • Razorpay — payment processing (their policy)
  • Google Fonts — font loading (no tracking)
  • Leaflet / OpenStreetMap — map display
  • Google Analytics — if enabled

4. How to Control Cookies

Browser Settings

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Opt-Out

  • Google Analytics: Google Analytics Opt-out
  • Ads: Digital Advertising Alliance

5. Impact of Disabling Cookies

  • Login may not work
  • Language preferences won't save
  • Payment processing may fail
  • Some features may not function

6. Cookie Retention

  • Session cookies: Deleted when browser closes
  • Persistent cookies: Up to 1 year
  • Payment cookies: As per Razorpay policy

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy. The "Last Updated" date at the top indicates the latest revision.
