1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Name — for personalizing your report
  • Date of Birth (DOB) — for astrological calculations
  • Time of Birth (TOB) — for Lagna and Bhava calculations
  • Place of Birth — for geographic coordinates (latitude/longitude)
  • Email Address — for account and communication
  • Phone Number / WhatsApp — for OTP and report delivery (optional)
  • Payment Information — processed by Razorpay (we do NOT store card details)

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address, browser type, device info
  • Pages visited, time spent
  • Cookies and similar technologies

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • Astrological calculations — to generate your Kundli, Dasha, Varga, and other reports
  • Report delivery — email and WhatsApp delivery of PDF reports
  • Payment processing — via Razorpay
  • Customer support — to respond to your queries
  • Service improvement — to enhance our platform
  • Legal compliance — to comply with applicable laws

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data with:

  • Razorpay — for payment processing (they have their own Privacy Policy)
  • WhatsApp Business API / Twilio — for report delivery (only if you opt-in)
  • Cloud Hosting Providers — for secure storage
  • Legal Authorities — only if legally required
⚠ Important: Your astrological data (DOB, TOB, Place) is used solely for report generation. It is not shared with any third party for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for:

  • Active account: As long as your account exists
  • Deleted account: 90 days from deletion request (for legal compliance)
  • Payment records: 8 years (as per Indian tax laws)
  • Reports: 5 years or until you request deletion

5. Your Rights (Under DPDP Act 2023)

You have the following rights:

  • Right to Access — Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Correction — Update incorrect information
  • Right to Erasure — Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — Opt-out anytime
  • Right to Grievance Redressal — Complain to our Grievance Officer
  • Right to Nominate — Nominate someone to exercise your rights

To exercise these rights, email: dashaenterprises21@gmail.com

6. Security Measures

  • HTTPS/SSL encryption for all data transmission
  • Bcrypt password hashing — passwords are never stored in plain text
  • Restricted database access — only authorized personnel
  • Regular security audits
  • PCI-DSS compliant payments via Razorpay

7. Cookies

We use cookies for:

  • Essential cookies — login sessions, CSRF protection

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. See our Cookie Policy for details.
