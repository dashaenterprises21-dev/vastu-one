Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with Vastu One. This Refund Policy explains when and how you can request a refund.

1. Subscription Refunds

Pro Plan
Full refund available within 7 days of purchase if you haven't downloaded more than 2 PDF reports. After 7 days, no refund is available.
Enterprise Plan
Custom refund terms are defined in your contract. Contact your account manager for details.

2. Product Refunds

Physical products (gemstones, yantras, rudraksha) can be returned within 14 days of delivery if:

  • Product is unused and in original packaging
  • Certificate of authenticity is intact
  • Return is initiated via our support portal

Return shipping costs are borne by the customer unless the product is defective.

3. Course Refunds

LMS courses can be refunded within 3 days of enrollment if less than 20% of the course has been completed. After 3 days, no refund is available.

4. Non-Refundable Items

  • PDF reports once downloaded
  • Personalized astrology consultations
  • Digital products (e-books, courses once completed)
  • Custom puja services

5. How to Request a Refund

Email refunds@vastuone.com with your order ID and reason for refund. We will respond within 48 hours. Approved refunds are processed within 7-10 business days to the original payment method.
