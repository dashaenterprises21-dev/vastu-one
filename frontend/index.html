Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about VASTU ONE.

Is VASTU ONE just an AI-generated Vastu report? +
No. VASTU ONE runs 54 deterministic engines against classical sources. AI is only used for explanation — never for generating sources or calculations. Every finding is traceable.
How are conflicting traditions handled? +
We don't merge traditions silently. Northern, Southern, and Modern interpretations are kept separate and surfaced explicitly. You choose the tradition for your client.
Is my property data private? +
Yes. VASTU ONE is multi-tenant isolated. Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We run continuous audit logging via Sentinel. No cross-tenant data access is possible.
Does AI generate Sanskrit citations? +
Never. Sanskrit citations come only from verified textual sources. AI is used for explanation and personalization — not for inventing sources.
Can I use VASTU ONE for commercial properties? +
Yes. We support residential, commercial, industrial, and land plots. Enterprise tier adds multi-property portfolio management and township analysis.
